###### 单词

group

词性：n.

习惯用法：a group of sth. （一组....）

A group of dogs are keeping guard at his door.

Do you have a group of keys in your pocket?

I couldn't bear that a group of people were talking so loudly!

Were a group of people were talking loudly and you couldn't bear it?

Look, the park is full of a groud of people.Why are they gathering there?



pop singer

词性：n.

He is a pop singer.

Is he a pop singer?

He was a pop singer.

Was he a pop singer?

Why isn't he a pop singer now?



club

词性：n.

词组：nightclub / tennis club

If you are in the nightclub,you can't pay attention.

I was in a nightclub.

We met each other in a nightclub last night.

Let's go to the tennis club.

The nightclub was cleaned yesterday.



performance

词性：n.

习惯用法：give a performance (演出动词形式： 表演：在公共场合或舞台上展示自己的才艺或技能。)

The pop singer will give us a wonderful performance on the stage tonight.

Last night, he couldn't give us a performance on that occasion immediately.

I saw a very wonderful performance last night.

The performance was great.

In that performance we had a very great seat.



occasion

词性：n.

adv.形式：occasionally

习惯用法：on this occasion

Are you sure about doing this on this occasion?

On this occasion he just stood on his head.

Why did he stand on his head on this occasion?

He will see her occasionally.

Why did he do that on this occasion?

###### Lesson13 The Greenwood Boys

The Greenwood Boys are a group of pop singers. At present, they are visiting all parts of the country. They will be arriving here tomorrow. They will be coming by train and most of the young people in the town will be meeting them at the station. Tomorrow evening they will be singing at the Workers’ Club. The Greenwood Boys will be staying for five days. During this time, they will give five performances. As usual, the police will have a difficult time. They will be trying to keep order. It is always the same on these occasions.

a group of sth. ( 一组... )

These dolls belong to him.
