###### Lesson 15  Good news

The secretary told me that Mr. Harmsworth would see me. I felt very nervous when I went into his office. He did not look up from his desk when I entered. After a few seconds he said, "Miss Eyre, you are late." I sat down and he still did not look at me. Then he said, "I am going to tell you some good news. We are going to increase your salary by 10%. You are doing very well, Miss Eyre." I was so surprised. I thought that perhaps I had misheard him. Then he said, "Of course, I hope you will still work for us. But from now on, you will get your salary once a month instead of once a week." I got up and said, "Thank you very much, Mr. Harmsworth." Then I left his office, still feeling a little confused.



The secretary told me that Mr. Harmsworth would see me. I felt very nervous when I went into his office. He did not look up from his desk when I entered. After I had sat down, he said that business was very bad. He told me that the firm could not afford to pay such large salaries. Twenty people had already left. I knew that my turn had come.
“Mr. Harmsworth,” I said in a weak voice.
“Don’t interrupt,” he said.
Then he smiled and told me I would receive an extra £1000 a year!
