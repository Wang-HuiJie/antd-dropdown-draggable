###### 如何表达

生某人的气 ( be mad at sb. )

出于某些原因 ( for some reason )

下定决心 ( be determined to do sth. )



###### 单词

mad

词性：adj.

习惯用法：be mad at sb.

He must have been mad at her last night.

She might be mad at somebody.

She was mad at me.

Do you think she will be mad at me?

He must have thought I was mad at him.



reason

词性：n.

习惯用法：for some reason.

For some reason,He is keen on fishing.

For some reason,that guy is keen in fishing.

For some reason,He always fishs for hours without catching anything.

Do you know what reason it is?

He must have been tired for some reason.



sum

词性：n.

习惯用法：a large sum of money.

That's a large sum of money.Why did you burn them all?

In sum, we must take a large sum of money to see him.

This sum of 3 and 5 is 8.

For some reason,He is interested in counting a large sum of money.

In sum,they spend the whole time in his room.



determined

词性：adj.

习惯用法：be determined to do sth.

He is determined to spend the whole morninig with me.

Does he determined to spend the whole morning with me?

He must determine some big decision.

He must have been determined to make a very big decision,mustn't he?

Why must he have been determined to make a very big decision?

###### Lesson21

Aeroplanes are slowly driving me mad. I live near an airport and passing planes can be heard night and day. The airport was built years ago, but for some reason it could not be used then. Last year, however, it came into use. Over a hundred people must have been driven away from their homes by the noise. I am one of the few people left. Sometimes I think this house will be knocked down by a passing plane. I have been offered a large sum of money to go away, but I am determined to stay here. Everybody says I must be mad and they are probably right.



###### 习惯用法

drive sb. mad（逼疯某人）

He might have driven her mad.

He drove him mad at the toilet yesterday.

The toilet must have driven him mad.

He will be driven mad.

Will he be driven mad?





come into use ( 投入使用 )

For some reason,it will come into use.

It might have been put into use.

Aeroplants will come into use in the airport tomorrow.

Will aeroplants come into use in the airport tomorrow?
What kind of aeroplants will into use in the airport tomorrow?



for some reason ( 基于某些原因 )

For some reason, he must have been mad.

For some reason, the airport will come into use in this airport next year.

He will come to see me for some reason tonight.

She might date me for some reason.

For some reason,they used a wooden arm instead of your arm.
