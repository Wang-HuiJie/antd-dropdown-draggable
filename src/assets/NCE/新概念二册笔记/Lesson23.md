###### 如何表达

你使我完整 （ You complete me ）

区 ( district )

吃惊 ( get a surprise )



###### 单词

complete

词性：v.

That bridge was completed in 2021.

It must be completed next year.

That road must have been completed in a few weeks.

We have to complete this bridge in twenty days.

Do you have to complete this bridge in twendy days?



modern

词性：adj.

We have a modern house in this street.

They must have taken a few weeks to complete this very modern house.

This must be a modern chair.

For no particular reason, we bought this modern glass.

For some reason, I am using this modern pen to write a letter to her.



strange

词性：adj.

That's feel so strange.

Do you feel strange?

I don't like that thing.It feels strange.

Does it feel strange?

What a strange guy!



district

词性：n.

Do you ever go to the Haidian district?

I always dream of having a big house in the Haidian district.

I am decided to buy a house in Haidian district.

He can't be determined to buy a house in Haidian District.

I thought of the houses in Haidian district last week. 



###### Lesson23

I had a letter from my sister yesterday. She lives in Nigeria. In her letter, she said that she would come to England next year. If she comes, she will get a surprise. We are now living in a beautiful new house in the country. Work on it had begun before my sister left. The house was completed five months ago. In my letter, I told her that she could stay with us. The house has many large rooms and there is a lovely garden. It is a very modern house, so it looks strange to some people. It must be the only modern house in the district.
