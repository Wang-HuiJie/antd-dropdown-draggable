###### 如何表达

梦想做某事 ( dream of doing sth. )

在我的年龄 ( at my age )

扔掉 ( throw away )



###### 单词

dream

词性：v.

习惯用法：dream of doing sth.

What's your dream?Watching TV all day at your small house?Is that your dream?That's so terrible.

That's not my dream.I always dream of going to Australia.

He dreams of catching some fish on this river every day.

I dreamt of watching TV all day in my room.

He must have been dreaming of becoming a wolf.



age

词性：n.

习惯用法：at sb.'s age

At my father's age, even a small thing is important.

It might be easy at your age.

That thing must have been diffcult at his age. 

All things will become easy at my age.

At his age,everything will become slow.



channel

词性：n.

In Lesson 13,Captain Alion has a small boat.He drives that boat across a large number of channels.

Do you always dream of sailing across a channel?

What kind of channel do you always dream of sailing across?

throw - threw - thrown

词性：v.

习惯用法：throw away ( 扔掉 )

For some reason,He just threw away his pencli.

After he had thrown his pencil,he felt very nervous.

He must throw away that stupid pen.

Must he throw away his stupid pen?

He must have thrown his stupid pen last week.



regularly

词性：adv.

 Both girls write to each other regularly now

Do both girls write to each other regularly now?

Why do both girls write to each other regularly now?

When do both girls write to each other regularly?

What kind of letter do both girls write to each other regularly?

Must both girls write to each other regularly now?



###### Lesson22

My daughter, Jane, never dreamed of receiving a letter from a girl of her own age in Holland. Last year, we were travelling across the Channel and Jane put a piece of paper with her name and address on it into a bottle. She threw the bottle into the sea. She never thought of it again, but ten months later, she received a letter from a girl in Holland. Both girls write to each other regularly now. However, they have decided to use the post office. Letters will cost a little more, but they will certainly travel faster.
