**单词**

exciting

词性：adj.

词组：exciting trip

造句：
What a exciting trip!

Do you ever have any exciting trips?

Last year, I went to New York. That was an exciting trip.

How exciting!

Do you feel excited?

Does she see that exciting movie?



receive

词性：v.

习惯用法：receive sth. from sb.

I receive a package from Japan.

Are you receive a package from Japan?

Yes, I received a package from Japan Last week.

What kind of package do you receive?

When I worked in that company, I received a photo from him every day.



firm

词性：n.

习惯用法：work for ( sb. / sw. )

例句：He is working for a big firm.

造句：

She was working for a big firm.

Did she work for a big firm?

When did she work for that big firm?

What kind of firm did she work for?

How many big firms did  she work for?



different

词性：adj.

习惯用法：A be different from B

造句：
That's different.

Is that different?

We have a different point of view.

Do we have any different points?

Why do you always have different points?



centre

词性：n.

习惯用法：in the centre of sw.

例句：China is in the centre of this map.

造句：

Beijing is the central city of China.

Is Beijing the central city of China?

Why is Beijing the central city of China?

How can Beijing be the central city of China?

Beijing is in the centre of China.



abroad

词性：adv.

造句：

I go abroad every year.

Do you go abroad every year?

How do you go abroad?

Why do you go abroad?

I was studying abroad.

### Lesson 4 An exciting trip

I have just received a letter from my brother, Tim. He is in Australia. He has been there for six months. Tim is an engineer. He is working for a big firm and he has already visited a great number of different places in Australia. He has just bought an Australian car and has gone to Alice Springs, a small town in the centre of Australia. He will soon visit Darwin. From there, he will fly to Perth. My brother has never been abroad before, so he is finding this trip very exciting.



**习惯用法**

receive sth. from sb. （收到来自某人的某物）

I have just received a book from my friend in the room.

It's a rainy day but I don't worry about that.Because I have just received an umbrella from my neighbour.

Why haven't you received the book yet?

I always receive my salary on the 12th of every month.

When do you receive your salary?



work for sb. （为某人工作）

People don't work with me,People work for me.

Who do you work for?

Who worked for you?

Did he work for you?

All the people in this company work for me.



a great number of sth. （大量的）修饰可数名词

a great deal of sth. （大量的）修饰不可数名词

We have a great number of people.Don't worry about that.

Listen,you have a great deal of money, but I don't have any coins so we can't be friends.

Do you have a great number of friends?

That's a great number of stones.

She has a great number of diamonds.



have gone to （去了但没回来）

have been to（去过又回来了）

I have gone to Japan.

I have been to U.S.A.

I never have been to Singapore.

Did you ever go to Singapore?

Have you ever been to Singapore?



going to sw. （将要去某地）

I am going to Shanxi for a trip.

Are you going to Shanxi?

We are going to Beijing right now!

Why are you going to Beijing now?

Where are you going to now?



fly to sw. （坐飞机去某地）

He flew to Japan last year with me.

Did he fly to Japan last year with you?

Where did he fly to last year with you?

When did he fly to Japan with you?

How did he to Japan with you?

I am flying to Japan.

He has flown to Japan.



find n. adj. （认为....）

He finds the whole thing very interesting.

I find this company very terrible.

Why do you find this company very terrible?

When did you find this company very terrible?

Do you find this company very terrible too?
